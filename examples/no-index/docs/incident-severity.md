# Incident severity

Sev-1 is a customer-facing outage or any data loss. It pages the on-call and the
incident commander immediately, at any hour.

Sev-2 is degraded service with a workaround available. It pages during business
hours only.

Sev-3 is internal or cosmetic. It becomes a ticket and pages nobody.

Severity is set by whoever declares the incident and can be raised later but
never lowered while the incident is open.
