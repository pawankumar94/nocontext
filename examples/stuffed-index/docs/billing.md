# Billing and invoicing

Invoices are issued on the first business day of the month for the month just
ended, and are due in thirty days.

Usage overages are billed in arrears at the contracted unit rate. There is no
overage on the free tier, requests are simply rejected once the allowance is
spent.

Annual contracts are invoiced up front. Mid-term upgrades are prorated to the
day. Downgrades take effect at renewal and are never refunded mid-term.
